One platform. One loop. One screen.

ISR outpaces the tools - and the tools are in the wrong place.

Operators plan in one tool, execute in another, and analyze in a third, and lose time to the hand-offs. The capable systems are heavy, costly, and hard to field at the edge; the commercial analysis services run off your network and cannot go forward, classified, or denied.

Watchglass fuses live full-motion video, the moving-map picture, detection, analytics and reporting into one screen, with a classification-aware routing layer so data only reaches connections cleared to carry it - offline and air-gap capable, from authorized or open feeds only.

  • One server, any browser - a ground-station laptop, a server, or a cloud instance; everyone else uses a browser.
  • Runs disconnected / air-gapped; resilient over Starlink, Starshield and cellular, and in denied comms.
  • Classification-aware by network route, end to end.
  • Authorized and open feeds only - not a collection or intercept tool.

An entire mission suite, not a point tool

From planning to execution to after-action - and back.

Four modes on one codebase: Demonstration, Training, Mission Planning, and Operations - closing the loop with no hand-offs.

Plan

Deliberate planning

TLP / MDMP to a five-paragraph OPORD and CONOP, a terrain-driven course-of-action recommendation, and a wargame.

Prepare

Rehearse in 3D

An animated 3D ROC-drill rehearsal - the operation plays out phase by phase over the terrain before execution.

Execute

Live operations

Promote the approved order in one step - graphics, collection plan, airspace and command-and-signal go live on the common operational picture.

Assess & save

After-action

Planned-versus-executed review, retained operation history, and federation so one operation's picture informs the next.

Why Watchglass

Owned, scalable, and built for the edge.

Owned and run by you

On your own network, including classified and the edge - not an off-network service and not a vendor cloud. No per-mission analyst dependency.

Scalable

One codebase from a single operator or ODA to a theater or national headquarters, which can watch many operations across commands at once through federation.

AI in your enclave

The analytic AI runs on the Anthropic API for unclassified, or on Amazon Bedrock in your own AWS / GovCloud for classified - the data never leaves the enclave.

Open standards, no lock-in

MISB / STANAG KLV, Cursor-on-Target / TAK, MIL-STD-2525 / APP-6, OGC API, GeoJSON / KML, ICD 203 - it interoperates with your existing tools.

Constantly learning

Learns each area's normal, source reliability, and your sensor layouts, and surfaces estimative warnings - every judgment logged and reversible.

Analytic rigor built in

ICD 203 estimative language, source provenance, and an audit trail throughout - products that stand up to scrutiny. Cueing, never positive identification.
