The Platform

One screen for the whole picture.

Watchglass fuses live full-motion video, the moving-map picture, detection, analytics, reasoning, and reporting into a single operational view - so the operator acts on one picture instead of stitching five tools together.

Live full-motion video

Every sensor, on the map.

Full-motion video plays alongside the moving map with the sensor footprint drawn live, so the screener always knows what the camera is looking at and where it sits in the world. MISB / STANAG KLV metadata drives the footprint, slews, and track history.

  • EO / IR feeds with live footprint and slew-to-cue.
  • Multi-channel - several feeds and screens at once.
  • KLV-driven geo-registration and track history.

Common operational picture

One map, every track and graphic.

Aircraft, ground tracks, control measures, airspace, and collection all live on a single 2D and 3D moving map. Symbology follows MIL-STD-2525 / NATO APP-6, and the picture is shared live across every connected operator.

  • 2D and 3D terrain anywhere in the world, offline-capable.
  • MIL-STD-2525 / APP-6 symbology and Cursor-on-Target / TAK.
  • Shared live - one picture for the whole team.

Detection, analytics & learned normal

Cueing that points the eye, never positive ID.

Detection, movement, and anomaly cues draw the screener to what is worth a look without cluttering the screen or claiming identification. Watchglass learns each area's normal and surfaces estimative warnings - every judgment logged, sourced, and reversible.

  • Movement and change cues - recall over precision, never a hidden candidate.
  • Learned-normal anomalies - flags what breaks the pattern of life.
  • Estimative, sourced, auditable - ICD 203 throughout.

AI reasoning in your enclave

An analyst's assistant that never leaves your network.

Ask the operation questions, triage OSINT for relevance, draft a SITREP, and get health and self-healing reasoning - all from an analytic AI that runs unclassified, or on Amazon Bedrock inside your own AWS / GovCloud for classified. The data never leaves the enclave.

  • Watch-floor Q&A and SITREP over the live operation picture.
  • OSINT relevance triage and analytics reasoning.
  • Runs in your enclave - Anthropic API or Bedrock / GovCloud.

Reporting & analytic rigor

Products that stand up to scrutiny.

Assessments carry ICD 203 estimative language, source provenance, and an audit trail from first cue to final report. After-action review plays the operation back - planned versus executed - and retains the history so the next operation starts smarter.

  • ICD 203 estimative language and confidence throughout.
  • Provenance and audit trail on every judgment.
  • After-action playback and retained operation history.
